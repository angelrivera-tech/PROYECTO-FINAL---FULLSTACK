ABOUT US AND OUR MISSION

We are a little team of 30 journalists with coding experience. We all got together to bring you the best way possible to digest news and understand it without getting lost, confused, or leaving with a wrongful idea of said news. Our mission is to make sure even the most poor and needy people are able to know the truths about what's happening around them without the bias from other people or sources confusing them.
